The level of investor interest in precious metals has retreated to the point where it has almost ceased to exist. It has been like this for months, indicated by the level of open interest in silver on Comex, which is the black line illustrated below: And no wonder: last May, open interest fell into deeply oversold territory, breaching levels last seen in September 2013. Confirming the almost total absence of investor interest, the managed money category which is comprised of hedge funds and similarly regulated investment managers has long contract interest still at record lows for the last twenty years.
