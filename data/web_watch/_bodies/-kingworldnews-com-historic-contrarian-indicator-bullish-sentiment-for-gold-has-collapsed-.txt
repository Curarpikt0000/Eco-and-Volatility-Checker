Russian Diesel Deal Ole Hansen, Head of Commodity Strategy at SaxoBank: As expected, Trump’s diesel deal with Putin has barely moved the needle, given the relatively small volumes involved. Both US ULSD and European gasoil cracks remain exceptionally elevated, highlighting the persistent shortage of refined products. The widening divergence between diesel and gasoline cracks on both sides of the Atlantic underscores that the current supply crisis remains overwhelmingly concentrated in middle distillates, which include jet and maritime fuels, and heating oil.
