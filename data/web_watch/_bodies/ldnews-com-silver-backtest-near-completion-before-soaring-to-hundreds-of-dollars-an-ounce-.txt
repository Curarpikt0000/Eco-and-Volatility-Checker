The silver bull market roadmap I have been following for the past couple of years is quite simple yet extremely powerful, and it continues to provide a valuable guide and source of encouragement even throughout the correction that began in January 2026. That roadmap is the massive cup-and-handle pattern that has been forming since the 1960s. Silver’s five-decade-long cup-and-handle pattern projects the metal reaching hundreds of dollars per ounce following a breakout, due to the sheer magnitude of the pattern in terms of both time and price . As with all cup-and-handle patterns, the breakout is triggered when the asset decisively clears the resistance level, or ceiling, that formed while the pattern was developing. In silver’s case, that critical resistance level was $50 per ounce. Far from being arbitrary, the $50 level marked the peak of both of silver’s previous secular bull markets, in 1980 and 2011, with each peak followed by a bear market lasting a decade or more, as I documented in this report . But something big and different happened one year ago, in the fall of 2025: silver finally blasted through that critical $50 ceiling, marking a genuine breakout from the massive cup-and-handle pattern.
