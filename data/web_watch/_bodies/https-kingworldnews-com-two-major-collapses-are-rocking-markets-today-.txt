Two Collapses Are Rocking Markets Today This Is Getting Serious The Kobeissi Letter: BREAKING: The Strategic Petroleum Reserve (SPR) fell -300,000 barrels last week, to 282.7 million barrels, the lowest level since October 1982. Since March 20th, US oil inventories in the SPR have dropped -132.8 million, or -32%. KING WORLD NEWS NOTE: US Strategic Oil Reserves Collapse Near Lowest Level In History We have now seen drawdowns in 29 of the last 30 weeks. For perspective, oil reserves in the SPR stood at 653.9 million barrels in 2020, or +131% above current levels. Meanwhile, commercial crude oil stocks excluding the SPR dropped -3.1 million barrels last week, to 412.5 million barrels, the lowest reading since the end of July. This is also ~3% below the 5-year average for this time of year. America’s oil stockpiles are at critically low levels. Housing Market The Kobeissi Letter: BREAKING: The NAHB Housing Market Index fell -3 points in October, to 29, its lowest level since June 2012. KING WORLD NEWS NOTE: US Homebuilder Sentiment Hits Lowest Level Since 2012! This marks the 2nd consecutive monthly decline in homebuilder sentiment, totaling -6 points, marking the largest 2-month decrease since June 2025. All 3 components deteriorated, with current sales falling -2 points, to 33, the lowest since June 2012. Sales expectations dropped -5 points, to 32, the lowest since November 2022, while buyer traffic plunged -5 points, to 18, the lowest since April 2012, excluding the 2020 pandemic. Furthermore, 43% of builders cut prices this month, the highest proportion of any month from 2020 to 2026. The affordability crisis is putting further pressure on homebuilder sentiment.
